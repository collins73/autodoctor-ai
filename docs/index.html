Frequently Asked Questions

What does the check engine light mean?

The check engine light means your car's computer detected a fault. It could be minor (loose gas cap) or serious (engine misfire). Rebel Auto Agent reads your OBD-II code and explains exactly what it means instantly.

How much does a car diagnostic cost?

Dealerships charge $100–$200 for a diagnostic. Rebel Auto Agent gives you the same information instantly for $19.99/month with unlimited diagnostics — or try 3 free.

What is an OBD-II fault code?

OBD-II codes (like P0420, P0300, P0171) are standardized codes your car generates when it detects a problem. We translate every code into plain English with severity and repair cost estimates.

Do I need an OBD scanner to use the app?

No hardware needed — just enter your code manually or describe your symptoms. The app also supports Bluetooth and WiFi OBD-II adapters for automatic scanning.

Is Rebel Auto Agent available on iPhone and Android?

Yes. The app works on any device via browser and can be installed to your home screen on both iOS and Android as a PWA.
